About

No shouting. Just the file.

Deep Account is an independent channel about American politics. We open case files from Washington and follow the evidence, record by record.

What you will find here

A hearing, a ruling, a sealed report: each video takes one event and lays it out as a case file. The evidence is presented as lettered exhibits, the dates go on a single timeline, and the questions the record cannot answer are left open instead of filled with guesses.

We are interested in what the documents and the official footage actually show. We would rather be slow and right than first and wrong.

What we are not

We are not a newsroom with reporters in the hearing room. We work from the public record, which anyone can check, and we list our sources so that you can.

Deep Account is not affiliated with any government body, political party or campaign.

How a file is built

  • No invented scenes or staged hearings.
  • No quotes without a source.
  • No allegation presented as a verdict.
  • Corrections published, not buried.
