CFTC Nominees Asked: Is a Contract on an NFL Game a Sports Bet?

On Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, the Senate Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry Committee, chaired by Sen. John Boozman (R-Ark.) with Sen. Amy Klobuchar (D-Minn.) as ranking member, heard two nominees to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission: Nathan Anonick, nominated for a Republican seat, and Matthew Rowland, nominated for a Democratic seat. The CFTC has had one member, Chairman Michael Selig, since December 2025. The question running through the hearing: is a prediction-market contract on who wins a football game a sports bet, which states and tribes regulate, or a federally regulated contract? Federal courts have not agreed. Asked by Sen. Adam Schiff (D-Calif.) about the Colts–Texans game, Mr. Anonick said federal courts have disagreed on that. Two days later, on Nov. 19, the Senate passed S. 4035, the DEATH BETS Act, on contracts tied to war, terrorism, assassination, U.S. military operations and a person's death; the House has not referred it to a committee. By its own text, the bill leaves the sports question where it was.

The timeline

  1. 2018The Supreme Court decides Murphy v. NCAA, striking down the federal law that barred states from authorizing sports betting.
  2. Nov. 19, 2025Michael Selig's confirmation hearing (S. Hrg. 119-214).
  3. Dec. 2025Mr. Selig is confirmed as CFTC chairman; since then he has been the commission's only member.
  4. Mar. 10, 2026Sen. Schiff introduces S. 4035, the DEATH BETS Act.
  5. Apr. 2026The Third Circuit, two to one, upholds an order blocking New Jersey from applying its gambling laws to the exchange Kalshi (preliminary stage).
  6. May 2026Minnesota enacts a law banning prediction market platforms; the CFTC goes to court to block it.
  7. Aug. 2026The Ninth Circuit rules for Nevada (preliminary stage).
  8. Sept. 2026The Sixth Circuit rules for Ohio and Tennessee (preliminary stage). On Sept. 30, Sen. Curtis becomes a cosponsor of S. 4035.
  9. Nov. 17, 2026Senate Agriculture Committee hearing on the nominations of Nathan Anonick and Matthew Rowland.
  10. Nov. 19, 2026The Senate passes S. 4035, as amended, by voice vote.
  11. Nov. 20, 2026The House receives S. 4035 and holds it at the desk; it has not been referred to a committee.

Open questions

  1. Will the committee vote on the nominees? As of Nov. 22, 2026, the committee has not scheduled a vote; the Senate holds brief pro forma sessions until Nov. 30.
  2. Will the House act on S. 4035? As of Nov. 22, 2026, the House has received the bill and held it at the desk; it has not been referred to a committee.
  3. Who sets the rules for a contract on a football game? The sports question remains with the courts and, if it chooses, with Congress; none of the appeals rulings so far is final on the merits.
  4. Will the CFTC or Chairman Selig respond to the hearing? As of Nov. 22, 2026, we have found no statement responding to it.
  5. What happens in January? A new Congress convenes; bills not passed by then must start over, and pending nominations are generally returned to the President.

Sources & method

How a file is built

Every video follows the same discipline. The structure changes with the story. The standard does not.

  1. The record

    We start from primary material: committee video, court orders, official reports and transcripts.

  2. The exhibits

    Each piece of evidence gets a letter. You always know what you are looking at and where it came from.

  3. The timeline

    Dates go on one line, so the sequence of events is clear before anyone interprets it.

  4. The open questions

    Where the record stops, we say so. Allegations are called allegations.
